## Overview:
Modern Machine Learning is shaped by the hardware used under the hood. It ranges from role-specific hardware such as NPU(Neural Processing Unit), LPU(Language Processing Unit), DPU(Data Processing Unit) to Core ML processors such as CPU, TPU and GPU. CPU, the brain of the computer is present in all the computers, primarily focused on the processing complex tasks. On the other hand GPU, are known for performing multiple small tasks parallely in order to accomplish a task much faster than a CPU could ever do. ML algorithms are repeatedly trained on huge datasets, so that we recognise the pattern hidden under them. Model training can sometimes take even days to finish. Hence, studying GPU can come handy to improvise model training and can save time too. This was my motivation behind learning GPU Programming. In this article, is my exploration on GPU programming so far, the challenges, learnings and key takeaways.
